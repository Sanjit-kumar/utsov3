2026 — The Journey

Every great celebration starts long before the lights go up. Here's what Team UTSOV has been building behind the scenes for this year's Durga Puja.

✓
Venue Secured
Jo Ann Magistro Performing Arts Center, East Brunswick — booked and confirmed for both days.
✓
Teams Assembled
Dozens of volunteers across food, logistics, marketing, and event-day operations — the people who make it all run.
✓
Artists Confirmed
Iman Chakraborty & Band, Sourendro–Soumyojit, and a full lineup of cultural performances locked in.
✓
Registration Opened
Passes, the Children's Art Competition, and sponsorship packages are all live and filling up.
●
Final Prep & Rehearsals
In progress now — decorations, sound checks, and last-minute logistics as we count down to event day.
