✦ Pick Your Inspiration

Art Themes Rooted
in the Legend of Maa Durga

Every theme is inspired by the real stories and symbols of Durga Puja. Young artists can pick whichever speaks to them most — there's no wrong way to celebrate the Goddess.

⚔️
Durga's Ten Mighty Arms
Each of Maa Durga's ten hands holds a divine weapon gifted by the gods — a trident, a sword, a lotus. Draw her in all her power.
🦁
The Great Battle
Maa Durga rides her lion into battle against the shape-shifting demon Mahishasura — nine days of courage ending in victory.
🏠
My Dream Pandal
If you could design any Durga Puja pandal in the world, what would it look like? Lights, colors, towers — go wild!
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
The Goddess's Family
Durga doesn't come alone — Lakshmi, Saraswati, Ganesh and Kartik all arrive together. Paint the whole divine family.
🥁
Dhaak, Dhunuchi & Dance
The booming dhaak drum, swirling dhunuchi smoke, and joyful dancing — capture the sounds and motion of the festival.
✨
Alpona Patterns
Beautiful hand-painted rice-paste designs decorate every puja doorstep. Create your own alpona pattern, big or small.
💭
Free Theme: What Puja Means to Me
No rules here — show us Durga Puja through your own eyes. Family, food, friends, fun — whatever makes it special to you.
