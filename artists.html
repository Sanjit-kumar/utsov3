Headliner — Oct 17
✦ Opening Night Headliner

Nandi Sisters

Bollywood & Folk Fusion

Rising stars of Indian music, the Nandi Sisters have taken both Bollywood and South Indian cinema by storm. Mentored by the legendary A.R. Rahman, their signature blend of Carnatic classical training, folk sensibility, and modern pop arrangements has earned them millions of fans across India and the diaspora.

Known for their viral Balcony Concert series and acclaimed performances at major music festivals, the Nandi Sisters bring raw emotional power, stunning vocal harmonies, and infectious joy to every stage they grace. UTSOV 2026 will be their New Jersey debut.

Headliner — Oct 18
✦ Grand Evening Headliner

Rupankar Bagchi

Bengali Folk & Modern

Rupankar Bagchi is the voice that every Bengali heart recognizes — his songs are woven into the fabric of Bengali life, from university corridors to kitchen radios to the most intimate moments of loss and love. His music bridges the river between traditional folk, Rabindrasangeet, and contemporary Bengali pop.

A National Award-winning artist with decades of acclaimed work across cinema and concerts, Rupankar's UTSOV performance promises to be an evening of extraordinary emotion — a celebration of Bengali identity and the unbreakable bond between Maa Durga's children and their homeland.

Grand Finale — Oct 18
✦ Grand Finale Act

Lakkhichhara &
Raju Das Baul

Baul & Folk Fusion

A rare and electric convergence of two forces: Lakkhichhara, the pioneering Bengali folk-rock band who have redefined how a generation hears their cultural roots; and Raju Das Baul, the living embodiment of the Baul mystic tradition — the wandering minstrels of Bengal whose music is pure soul.

Together, they create performances that transcend genre, language, and expectation. Raw, joyful, spiritually charged, and utterly transcendent — their UTSOV finale will be the night you never forget. The Baul philosophy is one of the Goddess herself: seek the divine through love, music, and surrender.

Our community programs feature extraordinary local and regional talent across both days.

Natya Mandir Dance Ensemble
Classical Indian Dance

New Jersey's premier Bharatanatyam and Odissi dance ensemble presents "Mahishasura Mardini" — a classical retelling of Durga's victory over the demon, set to live music.

🎻
Bengali Youth Orchestra
Fusion Classical

Young musicians aged 10–22 perform an original fusion composition blending Rabindrasangeet with Western orchestral arrangements. A stunning showcase of the next generation.

🥁
Dhak Masters
Traditional Bengali Percussion

Master dhaki players from Bengal lead the morning puja ceremonies and Dhunuchi Naach. Experience the thunderous, soul-stirring heartbeat of Durga Puja live.
