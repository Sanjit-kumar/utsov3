Our Mission

Celebration & Compassion

✦

No celebration is complete without giving back to the society and helping the less privileged of the world. Together, let's celebrate not only our culture and community, but also the power of kindness to transform lives.

As Utsov, our primary aspiration is to provide little relief to those who are in need for education, health support and social upliftment. We also contribute towards the cause for environmental development to make the world more greener for our future generations. Some portion of our contribution is reserved for any natural disasters during the year.

Please join us on this mission to bring smile to many faces. No one has ever become poor by giving.

2025 – 2026

This Year's Charity Partners

✦

Four extraordinary organizations transforming lives across India and New Jersey. Your UTSOV ticket supports every one of them.

🏫
STPN Trust — Education & Safety
📍 West Bengal, India

Supporting the STPN High School for Underprivileged & Orphan Children through critical infrastructure — including fire safety systems, security, and emergency evacuation equipment. Every child deserves a safe place to learn and grow.

⚕️
Humanity — Sundarbans Satellite Health Clinic
📍 Sundarbans Delta, West Bengal

A 4-bed medical facility in the remote Sundarbans delta serving thousands of patients with access to childbirth care and minor surgical procedures — bringing essential healthcare where none previously existed in this vulnerable coastal region.

📖
Nayantara — Tribal Education & Nutrition
📍 Birbhum District, West Bengal

Reaching 26 tribal villages in Birbhum District, Nayantara ensures children from the most marginalized communities attend school and receive nutritious daily meals. Education and nutrition together — because hungry children cannot learn.

💧
Mukti — Water, Health & Sanitation
📍 Rural West Bengal, India

Through three life-changing programs — Jal Hi Kal (clean water), Mutko Dhara (sanitation), and Swastha Shongini (mobile health) — Mukti addresses the foundational needs of rural communities that formal systems have left behind.

A Legacy of Giving

Prior Affiliated Organizations

✦

Over more than a decade, UTSOV's community has rallied behind causes stretching across continents — from flood-ravaged Kerala to hurricane-hit Texas, from hungry children in Bengal to isolated elderly in New York City.

🥗
Nourish NJ
📍 Central New Jersey

Addressing food insecurity across central New Jersey through meal distribution and community food programs for families in need.

🍱
Meals on Wheels — NYC
📍 New York City

Delivering nutrition and companionship to homebound elderly New Yorkers — ensuring no senior goes without a meal or a human connection.

➕
American Red Cross
📍 United States

First responders in disaster relief — UTSOV has rallied multiple community drives supporting Red Cross flood, hurricane, and emergency relief efforts across the country.

🎗️
American Cancer Society
📍 United States

Supporting life-saving cancer research and patient care programs that have touched millions of American families fighting this disease.

🌊
Hurricane Harvey Relief Fund
📍 Texas, USA

Emergency aid for survivors of the 2017 catastrophic Texas flooding — one of the most destructive hurricanes in US history, displacing over 30,000 people.

🌽
Feeding Florida
📍 Florida, USA

A statewide network of food banks ensuring food security for millions of Floridians facing hunger and economic hardship across the state.

⚕️
St. Jude Children's Research Hospital
📍 Memphis, Tennessee

Pioneering treatments for childhood cancer and serious diseases — where no family ever receives a bill for treatment, housing, travel, or food.

🤝
ADI — All India Relief Fund
📍 India

Providing emergency disaster relief and long-term rehabilitation for underserved communities across India following natural and humanitarian crises.

🌧️
Kerala Flood Relief Fund
📍 Kerala, India

Emergency support for survivors of the devastating 2018 Kerala floods — one of the worst natural disasters in the state's modern history, displacing over a million people.

🙏
Bharat Sevashram Sangha
📍 West Bengal & USA

A century-old humanitarian organization serving the destitute through food, shelter, and education programs across Bengal, India, and the diaspora worldwide.

🌱
Shrirampur Child Guidance Center
📍 Shrirampur, West Bengal

Supporting the emotional and developmental wellbeing of children with special needs and learning challenges in Shrirampur, West Bengal.

🏔️
Nepal PM's Disaster Relief Fund
📍 Nepal

Emergency aid and reconstruction support following the 2015 Nepal earthquake — a magnitude 7.8 disaster that claimed nearly 9,000 lives and left millions homeless.

🪔
Tapyasya
📍 India

A volunteer-driven initiative dedicated to holistic community development through education, healthcare, and grassroots social action in underserved regions.
