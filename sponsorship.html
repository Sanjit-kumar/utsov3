Proudly Presented by PC Chandra Jewellers

"যেমন সোনা দেবীকে সাজায়, তেমনি পিসি চন্দ্র সাজায় আমাদের প্রতিটি শুভমুহূর্ত।"
As gold adorns the Goddess, PC Chandra adorns every auspicious moment of our lives.

For generations, PC Chandra Jewellers has adorned Bengali celebrations — from wedding mandaps to Durga Puja pandals. As the title sponsor of UTSOV 2026, PC Chandra continues a partnership built on shared values: excellence, tradition, and the celebration of Indian heritage.

Just as gold has always been Durga's divine adornment, PC Chandra brings the brilliance of Bengali craftsmanship to every occasion. We are honored and grateful for their continued support.

Why Partner with UTSOV?

The UTSOV Advantage

✦
👥

Thousands of Attendees

UTSOV draws 1,000+ engaged South Asian families to a single venue over two days — an unparalleled opportunity to connect with the NJ Indian diaspora community in a deeply meaningful context.

🌐

Digital Reach of 5,000+

Our social media channels, email lists, and community networks reach over 5,000 South Asian households across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and beyond. Your brand, in front of your market.

❤️

Purpose-Driven Association

UTSOV is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Sponsoring UTSOV aligns your brand with cultural preservation, philanthropy, and community — values that deeply resonate with South Asian consumers.

🤝

10+ Years of Trust

A decade of consistent, high-quality events has built extraordinary trust and loyalty among our audience. UTSOV sponsors are not just vendors — they are partners who are welcomed into our community family.

📺

Media Coverage

UTSOV receives coverage in South Asian media, local NJ news, and community publications. Your brand gains visibility beyond the event through organic media reach and community word-of-mouth.

💰

Tax Deductible

As a 501(c)(3) non-profit, all sponsorship contributions to UTSOV are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by US law. Your investment in community is rewarded at the balance sheet too.
