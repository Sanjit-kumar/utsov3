✦ Opening Night Headliner

Iman Chakraborty

Rabindra Sangeet & Bengali Folk

National Award-winning voice who has captivated audiences across the globe with her soulful singing and electrifying stage presence. Renowned for her beautiful blend of Rabindra Sangeet, Bengali folk, and contemporary music, Iman promises an unforgettable evening of melody, nostalgia, and celebration, bringing the vibrant spirit of Bengal to the UTSAV stage on October 17, 2026.

More Performances

✦

Our community programs feature extraordinary local and regional talent across both days.

💃
Natya Mandir Dance Ensemble
Classical Indian Dance

New Jersey's premier Bharatanatyam and Odissi dance ensemble presents "Mahishasura Mardini" — a classical retelling of Durga's victory over the demon, set to live music.

🎻
Bengali Youth Orchestra
Fusion Classical

Young musicians aged 10–22 perform an original fusion composition blending Rabindrasangeet with Western orchestral arrangements. A stunning showcase of the next generation.

🥁
Dhak Masters
Traditional Bengali Percussion

Master dhaki players from Bengal lead the morning puja ceremonies and Dhunuchi Naach. Experience the thunderous, soul-stirring heartbeat of Durga Puja live.
