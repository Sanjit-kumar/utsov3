Headliner — Sat, Oct 17
✦ Opening Night Headliner

Iman Chakraborty

National Award-Winning Playback Vocalist

An Oscar-shortlisted, National Award-winning voice who has captivated audiences from India to the diaspora stages of the US, UK, and Middle East. Iman's live sets move effortlessly across genres — timeless Tagore classics, chart-topping Bollywood and film playback, and soulful contemporary originals — performed with her full live band. Whatever language you grew up singing in, there's a song in her set that will feel like home. Catch her unforgettable evening of melody, nostalgia, and celebration on Saturday, October 17th, 2026.

Headliner — Sun, Oct 18
✦ Grand Evening Headliner

Sourendro–Soumyojit

Classical–Contemporary Fusion Duo

Pianist Sourendro Mullick and vocalist Soumyojit Das have spent two decades weaving Indian classical roots into bold, contemporary sound — sharing the stage with legends like Asha Bhosle, Kavita Krishnamurti, and Rekha Bhardwaj across more than a thousand concerts worldwide. Their sets move seamlessly between beloved Bollywood hits, timeless Tagore compositions, and popular numbers in Hindi and Bengali alike — a musical language built for every ear in the room, not just one. Join them on Sunday, October 18th, 2026 for an evening of soul-stirring melodies and masterful musicianship.
