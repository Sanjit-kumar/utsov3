Join the Seva

Volunteer
with UTSOV

UTSOV is built by community, for community. Every year, dozens of dedicated volunteers are the invisible force that makes two days of magic possible — from welcoming guests at the door to managing the puja queue, coordinating the mela, and ensuring every family feels at home.

Volunteering with UTSOV is seva — selfless service in the spirit of the Goddess herself. It is also a lot of fun.

🧡
Pure Joy
Seeing thousands of families celebrate because of your effort — that feeling is real and it stays with you.
⭐
Inner Circle
Work side by side with the UTSOV core team. You'll know exactly how the magic is made.
🫂
Your Tribe
UTSOV volunteers come back year after year — not for perks, but for each other. You'll find lifelong friends here.
🌱
Grow for Real
Coordination, hospitality, crowd management, stage logistics — hands-on skills you carry long after the festival ends.

Getting Here

🚗

Parking

Ample free parking is available on-site at the venue. No reservation needed.

🚌

Public Transport

The venue is accessible via NJ Transit. Nearby bus stops connect to major transit hubs. Check njtransit.com for routes and schedules.

♿

Accessibility

The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with designated parking, ramps, and accessible seating in the auditorium. Please contact us in advance if you need any additional assistance.

🕐

Gates Open

Both days, gates open at 9:00 AM. Morning puja begins at 9:30 AM. Evening concerts begin at 5:30 PM. Full schedule on the Events page.
