Our Mission

Celebration & Compassion

✦

UTSOV was founded on a simple truth: that the greatest offering we can make to Maa Durga — the Divine Mother who shelters every living being — is to shelter and uplift her children. Every ticket purchased, every sponsorship received, every dollar donated flows directly into causes that nourish lives.

We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Every year, our community unites not just to celebrate, but to serve. The festival is the celebration — the philanthropy is the prayer.

"She who protects the universe carries her strength in compassion. We carry ours in community."

🍽️

Nourishment

Feeding the hungry in NJ and partnering with food banks and shelters serving Indian diaspora communities.

🏫

Education

Funding scholarships, school supplies, and literacy programs for underprivileged children in West Bengal and New Jersey.

🏥

Healthcare

Supporting mobile health clinics and healthcare access initiatives for underserved rural communities in Bengal and NJ.

🌱

Community Resilience

Disaster relief for cyclone and flood survivors in Bengal, and emergency community support in New Jersey.

Who We Support

Organizations We Stand With

✦

These are the organizations and causes that UTSOV's community has rallied behind — year after year, with growing love and commitment.

❤️
Durga Puja Cyclone Relief Fund
West Bengal, India

Emergency relief for cyclone-affected families in coastal Bengal. UTSOV has raised over $25,000 for survivors of Cyclone Amphan and subsequent storms — providing food, shelter materials, and school supplies.

🏥
Community Health NJ
New Jersey, USA

Partnering with Community Health NJ to bring free health screenings, blood pressure checks, diabetes awareness, and wellness resources to the South Asian community in the tri-state area.

📚
Vidyashree School Fund
Bankura District, West Bengal

Sponsoring education for 50+ underprivileged children in rural Bengal — providing school fees, textbooks, uniforms, and mid-day meals for an entire academic year, funded by UTSOV donations.

🍛
NJ Community Food Bank
Central New Jersey, USA

Every year, UTSOV volunteers distribute food packages and cooked meals to New Jersey families in need. The act of sharing bhog — sacred food — extends beyond the puja grounds into our neighbors' homes.

👩‍🏫
BOAS NJ Women's Scholarship
New Jersey, USA

In partnership with BOAS NJ, we fund merit-and-need scholarships for young South Asian women pursuing higher education. Empowering women is honoring Durga — the supreme manifestation of Shakti.

🌿
Sundarbans Mangrove Project
Sundarbans, West Bengal

Supporting reforestation and ecological restoration in the Sundarbans — the mangrove forest that protects Bengal's coastal communities. Caring for Maa Durga's earth is an act of devotion.
